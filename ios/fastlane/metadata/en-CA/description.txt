Built for the Canadian small business owner.

GrowthPilot AI is a local-first back office for Canadian SMBs — bank and accounting reconciliation, receipt/invoice scanning, and B2B vendor tools, designed around Canadian tax rules instead of bolted on afterward.

WHY GROWTHPILOT AI

- CRA-Ready Tracking: Automatic GST/HST/PST extraction from scanned receipts and invoices, with BC and Ontario tax rules built in.
- Local-First Privacy: Your financial data is processed and stored on your device first — not centralized in someone else's cloud.
- B2B Vendor Matching: Find and compare local vendors, with price benchmarking against your region.
- Bank & Accounting Sync: Connect your bank (Plaid) and accounting platform (Xero, QuickBooks Online) to keep your books current automatically.
- Business Chat: Negotiate with buyers and vendors in an encrypted, in-app chat built for B2B deals.

DESIGNED FOR CANADA

GrowthPilot AI is built around Canadian business realities — GST/HST/PST tax handling, Canadian bank and accounting integrations, and a local-first data model aligned with Canadian privacy expectations.

Whether you're in Surrey, Burnaby, Toronto, or anywhere in between, GrowthPilot AI is built to work the way Canadian small businesses actually operate.
